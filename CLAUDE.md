## Reporting style
When reporting to me:
- Extremely concise. Sacrifice grammar for concision.
- Always reply in bullet points.
- Clearly highlight any issues/errors and next action items.

## New repos
- Create any new repo as a new top-level folder in `agent-foundry` (monorepo of many repos, `../agent-foundry`). Never a standalone repo.
- All future work happens there.
